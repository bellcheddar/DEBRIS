Why this matters

Plastic particles have now been detected in human blood, lung, placenta, artery plaque and brain. What that means for health is spread across thousands of papers in journals that rarely cite one another, so no one can say in one place what is actually known.

DEBRIS asks every abstract the same questions (which plastic, which organ, which mechanism, what kind of study, how close to an effect in people) and maps the answers, showing what has been seen in people, what only in cells, and where nobody has looked. An empty region means no published work, not safety, and nothing here claims plastics cause disease in people.
