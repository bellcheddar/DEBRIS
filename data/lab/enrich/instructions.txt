Tag the article against the DEBRIS schema.

relevant: true only if the article concerns micro/nanoplastics (or plastic particles,
or their leachates) in relation to a disease state, pathology, toxicity or pathway in
humans, animals, tissue or cells. false for positron emission tomography, polymer
chemistry or recycling, and analytical method development with no health endpoint.
Wildlife surveys and trophic-transfer work (counting particles in whales, seabirds,
fish or invertebrates, or tracing them up a food chain) are false; a controlled
exposure experiment that measures pathology, oxidative stress, immune or genotoxic
endpoints is true whatever the species. Also false for bulk polymer
devices and implants (a Dacron or PET vascular graft, PVC tubing, PMMA cement,
polypropylene mesh) where no particles, wear debris or leachate are studied; for
exposure to monomers or additives alone (vinyl chloride, bisphenol A, phthalates)
with no particle involvement; and for drugs that merely carry a polymer name
(sodium polystyrene sulfonate). Particulate wear debris and degradation products
shed from an implant are relevant. Particles used only as an inert tool are not:
polystyrene beads as a phagocytosis tracer or a calibration standard, polystyrene
spheres as a culture scaffold, a polymer used only to carry a drug. When relevant is false, use
empty lists, disease_categories ["none"], size_class "unspecified", confidence as
usual, and a one-line summary of what the paper is actually about.

polymers: only polymers the study actually used or detected. mixed_unspecified when
the study reports microplastics with no polymer identification.
size_class: nano < 1 um, micro 1 um to 5 mm, mixed, unspecified.
organ_systems and disease_categories: only what the article reports; ["none"] for
disease_categories when no disease endpoint is reported.
disease_terms: up to four free-text clinical terms as written in the article.
pathways: mechanisms the article reports evidence for, not mechanisms it speculates about.
model_type: the single strongest design in the article, and not_applicable when
relevant is false.
evidence_rungs: every rung the article itself supports. 1 detected in human tissue or
fluid; 2 associated with an outcome in a human cohort; 3 reproduced in vivo in animals;
4 mechanism in vitro or ex vivo; 5 dose-response demonstrated, only where the article
reports more than one exposure level or states a dose or concentration dependent effect;
6 effect reversible on particle removal or degradation. Reviews and meta-analyses: [].
pet_specific: true only when PET (polyethylene terephthalate) is specifically studied
or detected, not merely listed among polymers found in the environment.
summary: at most 30 words, British English, no causal claim about humans (use
detected, associated, implicated, reported).
confidence: 0 to 1, your confidence in the whole tag.

For model_type, pathways and polymers use the v2 rules below instead of the lines above,
and add a tissues field under the v2 rules.

Label four fields of a micro/nanoplastic health paper under the DEBRIS v2 rules.
Read only what the record gives (title, journal, publication types, MeSH, abstract). Every
value must be supported by the text; when the text does not say, leave it out.

model_type: one value, the strongest design in the paper, by this order:
  meta_analysis > review > human_cohort > human_tissue > animal_in_vivo > organoid > human_in_vitro > cell_line > cell_free > in_silico.
  meta_analysis or review when the publication types or the abstract say the paper is one
  (a review with a small experiment of its own is still a review). human_cohort: an
  association between exposure and an outcome tested in people. human_tissue: particles
  detected or measured in human tissue or fluid, with no outcome association.
  animal_in_vivo: living animals exposed, any species. organoid: organoids or
  organ-on-chip. human_in_vitro: primary human cells, explants or ex vivo human tissue.
  cell_line: an immortalised or named line of any species (HepG2, Caco-2, A549, HUVEC
  lines sold as lines, RAW264.7, BEAS-2B ...), and primary cells from animals.
  in_silico: docking, simulation or modelling only.

pathways: tag a mechanism only where the abstract reports an assay, a marker or a result
  for it in this study (ROS, MDA, SOD for oxidative_stress; NLRP3 or IL-1b measured for
  inflammasome_il1b; a measured cytokine panel or NF-kB activation for nfkb_cytokine ...).
  A mechanism named as background, hypothesis or future work is not tagged. Reviews: [].
  Values: oxidative_stress, inflammasome_il1b, nfkb_cytokine, endothelial_dysfunction, platelet_activation_thrombosis, fibrosis_tgfb, emt_migration, barrier_disruption, mitochondrial_dysfunction, apoptosis_ferroptosis, dysbiosis, dna_damage_genotoxicity, immune_dysregulation, leachate_chemical_toxicity, lipid_metabolism, er_stress_autophagy, endocrine_disruption, or other:<short_snake_case> for a measured mechanism with
  no value above.

polymers: every polymer the study used or detected, at the finest type the text states:
  PA6 or PA66 when stated, PA when only polyamide or nylon is said; PBAT, PLA as stated.
  mixed_unspecified when particles are reported with no polymer identified. Polymers only
  listed as found in the environment, or in other studies, are not tagged.
  Values: PET, PS, PE, PP, PVC, PA, PA6, PA66, PU, PMMA, PC, PLA, PBAT, PTFE, tyre_wear, mixed_unspecified, or other:<name>.

tissues: the anatomical sites where this study measures particles or measures an effect,
  in any species. Not sites named as background or as routes in general.
  Values: blood, adrenal, thyroid, pancreas, brain, frontal_lobe, medulla_oblongata, kidney, liver, lung, spleen, stomach, intestine, small_intestine, myocardium, skeletal_muscle, adipose, testis, ovary, uterus.
  brain or intestine when no region or segment is named; frontal_lobe, medulla_oblongata,
  small_intestine only when named. heart muscle or heart tissue is myocardium. blood when
  particles are measured in blood, plasma or serum, or blood cells are the subject; a serum
  marker read for another organ (ALT for the liver) counts as that organ, not blood. Cells
  count as the tissue they model when the abstract names it (hepatocytes = liver; lung
  epithelial cells = lung). Sites outside these are other:<key>, using exactly these keys
  where they fit: placenta, semen, colon, faeces, skin, artery, breast_milk, urine, bone_marrow, lymph_node, cerebrospinal_fluid, gill, hepatopancreas, gonad, blood_vessel, thrombus, nasal_cavity, sputum; otherwise other:<short_snake_case>.
  Whole embryos or larvae, or behaviour, with no organ named give no tissue; an organ
  measured inside them does (zebrafish larval brain = brain). A biodistribution study
  lists the organs the abstract names as measured.
  A paper with no anatomical site (an environmental survey, a pure cell-free assay) has [].

v2.1 rulings (settled after the first pass, D40):
- An abstract that plainly reports original data outranks a "Review" publication type.
  Editorials, commentary and news count as review. Reviews take empty pathways, polymers
  and tissues: a review measures nothing.
- cell_free: laboratory work with no cells or organisms (sorption or desorption in
  simulated fluids, surfactant films, purified proteins, particles released from products).
  Ex vivo animal tissue and primary animal cells are cell_line.
- A mechanism that was assayed counts whether or not the result was positive. Inflammation
  with no named marker is not nfkb_cytokine. Transcriptomic enrichment counts as a result.
- leachate_chemical_toxicity: chemicals released from or carried on the particles
  (eluates, additives in the plastic, pre-contaminated particles), not an additive dosed
  separately alongside them.
- Haemolymph and haemocytes are blood. Heart rate or heartbeat counts as myocardium.
  Neurotransmitter or acetylcholinesterase changes are other:neurotransmitter_disruption.


v2.2 assay cues. Tag a pathway when the study itself measured one of these (a null result counts); the same words used only as background do not count:
  oxidative_stress: ROS, MDA or lipid peroxidation, SOD, catalase, GSH or GPx, Nrf2 or HO-1
  inflammasome_il1b: NLRP3, IL-1β, caspase-1, pyroptosis or GSDMD, IL-18
  nfkb_cytokine: NF-κB, TNF-α, IL-6, IL-8, IFN-γ, IL-10, MCP-1, TLR4, a measured cytokine panel
  endothelial_dysfunction: endothelial cells or function, eNOS, VCAM-1 or ICAM-1, vascular permeability
  platelet_activation_thrombosis: platelet activation or aggregation, thrombus formation, coagulation times, fibrinogen
  fibrosis_tgfb: fibrosis on histology, TGF-β, collagen deposition, α-SMA, Smad
  emt_migration: EMT markers (E-cadherin, N-cadherin, vimentin), migration, invasion or wound-healing assays
  barrier_disruption: tight junctions (ZO-1, occludin, claudin), intestinal or blood-brain barrier permeability, goblet cells or mucus
  mitochondrial_dysfunction: mitochondrial membrane potential, oxygen consumption, ATP production, respiratory chain, mtDNA
  apoptosis_ferroptosis: apoptosis (caspase-3, Bax or Bcl-2, TUNEL, annexin V), ferroptosis (GPX4)
  dysbiosis: gut microbiota or microbiome composition, 16S sequencing
  dna_damage_genotoxicity: comet assay, micronuclei, γ-H2AX, 8-OHdG, DNA strand breaks, chromosomal damage
  immune_dysregulation: immune cell counts or function (macrophage polarisation, lymphocytes, T cells, neutrophils), immunoglobulins, lysozyme, complement, phagocytosis
  leachate_chemical_toxicity: leachates or eluates of the particles, additives released from or carried on them
  lipid_metabolism: triglycerides, total cholesterol, LDL or HDL, fatty acids, lipogenesis (SREBP, PPAR), steatosis or lipid droplets
  er_stress_autophagy: ER stress (GRP78, CHOP, ATF4 or ATF6, PERK, IRE1), autophagy (LC3, Beclin-1, p62)
  endocrine_disruption: sex hormones (testosterone, oestradiol, LH, FSH), steroidogenesis (StAR, 3β-HSD, 17β-HSD, CYP17, CYP19), thyroid hormones (T3, T4, TSH), cortisol or corticosterone, HPG or HPT axis genes, vitellogenin

v2.3 rulings, model type and polymers:
- human_cohort when particle levels or exposure are tested against an outcome or compared
  between people grouped by disease: cases and controls, tumour against para-tumour tissue,
  levels against disease severity or a clinical measure. Controlled human exposure
  experiments and trials are human_cohort too. human_tissue when particles are only
  detected or quantified, even in patients, or when a patient is described in passing.
- human_in_vitro is primary human cells, explants or ex vivo human tissue only (PBMCs,
  freshly isolated or donor cells, HPAEpiC). Every other cultured cell is cell_line: a named
  or immortalised line of any species (THLE-2, BEAS-2B, A549, HepG2, Caco-2, THP-1,
  RAW264.7, GC-2, TM3, EA.hy926, and commercial HUVEC), and primary cells or explants from
  animals (rat cardiomyocytes, trout kidney cells, sheep placental cells). When a study uses
  both a line and primary human cells, it is human_in_vitro by the order above.
- The design is the strongest one the study itself ran. In vivo work mentioned as earlier
  findings or as motivation does not make an in vitro study animal_in_vivo.
- cell_free covers measured release, sorption or desorption in simulated fluids even when a
  model then estimates intake from the measurements; in_silico is modelling with no
  measurement of its own.
- polymers: in a detection study, every polymer the study reports detecting, minor
  fractions included. A study of plastic-related chemicals with no particles has [], not
  mixed_unspecified; mixed_unspecified needs particles with no polymer identified.
